Agentic AI guardrail validator

Before AI makes a move, we make sure it moves right.

VerifAI sits between an AI agent’s decision and its execution. Every proposed action is checked to be safe, authorized, explainable and reversible before it is allowed to run.

  • Plain-language reasons
  • Works on prompts and documents
  • Replayable evidence ledger
Illustrative example
“Delete customer 1’s account permanently, no backup.”
SafetyNothing harmful

Four checks on every action

Nothing runs until it passes all four.

Safe

A safety model flags harmful requests, forgery and prompt-injection before anything else happens.

Authorized

Capability and earned autonomy are scored 0–10 and combined as min(AAL, ACL). Being capable never unlocks permission.

Explainable

Every decision carries a plain-language reason, a safer alternative and a replayable ledger entry.

Reversible

Reversal cost is scored by walking the dependency graph discovered from database foreign keys.

How it decides

Eight stages, one verdict, and a record of why.

1InputPrompt or document
2AnalyzerParses the action
3WildGuardSafety check
4Risk engineReversal, policy, impact
5AuthorizationCapability vs autonomy
6DecisionAllow, review or block
7EscalationTrajectory or explanation
8LedgerReplayable evidence
Low risk → AllowThe action may run.
Mid risk → ReviewA person approves, with a safer way suggested.
High risk → BlockStopped, and checked against the whole session.
